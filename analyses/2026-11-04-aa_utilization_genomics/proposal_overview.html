Comparative genomics across 45 strains · Researcher: Dikla Aharonovich · Plan drafted 2026-11-04 · KG release 0.1.0-alpha.9

The question

Which Prochlorococcus strains carry the genes to take up each amino acid and to strip its nitrogen off, and how does this differ between clades? A standalone, descriptive map of capacity: no hypothesis, no formal statistics. Strains share ancestry, so they aren't independent samples. Gene presence ≠ proven function.

Two questions per strain × amino acid (all 20)

① Can it take the amino acid up?

amino acid outside→transporter→inside the cell

Three evidence lines, kept separate:

  • TCDBcurated transporter system naming the amino acid
  • GOtransport function from a term list you approve
  • SBPpublished substrate-binding-protein prediction (42 of 45 strains)

Judged as whole systems: binding protein, permease and ATPase subunits recorded. A lone subunit is not a transporter. Exporters are flagged and not counted.

Calls: specific uptake · class-only ("amino acid") · SBP-only · export-only · none found

② Can it strip the nitrogen off?

amino acid→N-releasing enzyme→NH₄⁺ or glutamate
  • Release as ammonium: deaminases, ammonia-lyases, amino-acid dehydrogenases and oxidases, amidases
  • Transfer: aminotransferases hand the amino group to 2-oxoglutarate, making glutamate

Enzymes from a catalogue you approve (KEGG enzyme IDs, checked against KEGG). Enzymes also used to build amino acids are flagged "shared": weaker evidence.

Context: the full carbon-skeleton route, scored as steps present (k of n).

How we'll know the method works

Must be found

  • glnA (glutamine synthetase) in all 45 strains: tests the gene-matching
  • 2–3 aminotransferases that amino-acid building needs (e.g. ilvE), in all 45 strains: tests the N-transfer catalogue
  • MED4 urea transporter (urtA–E) returned when searching for urea: tests the transporter extraction

Must come out empty

  • Urea transporter (urtA–E) and phosphate transporter (pstSCAB) in MED4: no amino-acid uptake call
  • A route of only "shared" enzymes is never called complete in the dedicated count
  • One degradation route, named in advance as expected absent

Findings, not failures

  • All 45 strains look the same
  • Prochlorococcus carries only "shared" enzymes
  • Most amino acids: no route found

Each would be real biology, reported as a result.

Caveats that travel with every result

  • Annotation ≠ function. A gene matching an enzyme family is not proof of activity or direction.
  • Absent may mean "not sequenced." The KG has no genome-completeness field. NCBI assembly status is used to flag absences in incomplete genomes.
  • Small clades (HLIII, HLIV, LLIII) can't stand for their clade.
  • Evidence lines aren't independent. TCDB and GO calls often come from the same homology search.
  • No heterotroph check. Without one, we can't prove the method would find a dedicated breakdown route where one surely exists. "Not found" is reported with that limit.
  • Early look: MED4 results are seen before the rules are locked. This is recorded in the plan notebook.
