What it means for the question

The test works: MED4 senses N shortage and switches on glnA and urea genes strongly.
Amino-acid genes stay off. MED4 doesn't seem to reach for free amino acids when it needs nitrogen.
Over weeks, it switches on recycling and peptide genes: organic N from its own proteins, or peptides, not free amino acids.
Can't tell yet: no amino acids were added (genes may switch on only when their substrate is present) · MED4 only, so no strain comparison · gene on ≠ uptake proven
