What the pilot changed

  • Transporters set aside: MED4 had no amino-acid-specific uptake system in the database (0 transporter-database matches; 1 gene-function label, gltS).
  • Score whole pathways on breakdown-only steps, and require the step that frees the nitrogen.
  • Match by enzyme ID, never gene name: e.g. a gene named aspA is an aspartoacylase; PMM1468 (bioF) carries a threonine enzyme ID, so it's flagged.
  • 13 enzyme IDs not in the database (mostly Phe, Tyr, Trp) are to be checked against KEGG.
