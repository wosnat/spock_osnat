# Proposal critical review — first pass (interpretation)

Reviewer: `proposal-critic`, cold read of `proposal.md` (context: `decisions_log.md`, `spike/step2_med4_inventory.csv`). It used experiment-level KG facts only and looked up no hli expression data.

Dispositions are **proposed**, awaiting the researcher's choice.

| # | Severity | Finding (short) | Proposed disposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Blocker | "Responds to non-light stress" is not the same as "unrelated to light". Every non-light stress was run in the light, and nutrient limitation makes the same light relatively excessive. In MED4, only the phage-lysate dark vs light pair (and DCMU) bear on light-independence. | **Accept.** Relabel the broad reading "induced across stresses (light-dependence not tested)". Add the limit. Name the phage dark/light pair as the only direct light-independence check. |
| 2 | Concern | DCMU + light vs light is a photosynthetic electron-transport contrast, not more-vs-less light. Five of the six light microarrays share one dark control, and a majority vote by study would collapse the wavelength and DCMU contrasts. | **Accept.** Give DCMU its own row. Show the six Steglich conditions side by side, with no vote within that study. Note the shared dark control. |
| 3 | Concern | The "<20 changed genes" rule will flag most light microarray and phage/constant-dark tests, erasing the wavelength pattern and most of the light-independence check. | **Accept.** Count the flagged tests per group (experiment-level counts) before running. List in Known limits the stress types left with no testable contrast. Show gene-level calls for flagged tests. |
| 4 | Concern | "Up at any time point" favours experiments with many time points, and "changes in nearly everything" is undefined. Extended light/dark have about 50–65% of genes significant, which leaves Fisher little power. | **Accept.** Report significant time points out of total, and the direction mix. Define "changes in nearly everything" as more than 30% of measured genes significant in that direction at that time point. For those tests, show the hli fraction next to the genome fraction. |
| 5 | Concern | Chaperones and oxidative-stress genes are themselves light/redox-responsive. Several significant-only tables hold none of the comparison genes. There is no rule for a failed comparison check. Random sets are not size-matched. | **Accept.** Label chaperones and oxidative stress "general-stress references (themselves light-responsive)". If the references don't behave as expected in a stress type, report it as "reference uninformative". Size-match the random sets to the number of hli genes measured per table. |
| 6 | Concern | Fisher assumes independence, but hli paralogs are near-identical, so their calls are correlated and p-values inflated. The BH family was not specified. | **Accept.** Add an ortholog-group-level count (14 groups; a group counts as changed if any member changes) next to every gene-level test. State the inflation caveat. Use a separate BH family for each gene set, with the random sets outside them. |
| 7 | Concern | The background labels may be backwards. For "significant in any condition" tables the platform background is near-exact, while the in-table background biases against shared responders. The phage microarray table may be up-only. | **Accept.** Use the platform gene list where the KG has it (to check), and describe what each background conditions on. Mark the down tests for an up-only table "not testable". (Up-only is unverified; check in methods.) |
| 8 | Concern | Unlisted confounders: constant dark/light arms run on separate days; extended light/dark are time-of-day contrasts; 45 min vs days timescales; light microarrays are light onset, not excess light. | **Accept.** Add all four to Known limits. Call the light deliverable "light onset / photoperiod", not "light stress". |
| 9 | Concern | Of the proposed other strains, only NATL2A has light/dark transcript data. MIT0604 UV shock (all genes) is the closest excess-light test and is excluded by the selection rule. | **Accept.** The other-strain look covers breadth across stresses only. Add MIT0604 UV shock as a named check, and include the NATL2A darkness experiments. |
| 10 | Note | The orientation step depends on the extended-light sign being right. | **Accept.** In methods, check the sign of the extended light/dark contrasts on photosystem II genes before hli is read. |
| 11 | Note | The non-stress group may be stress relief, not neutral. | **Accept in part.** Keep the group, because it still shows whether hli changes in nearly everything. Label it "non-stress / possible relief", and don't call it a negative control. |
| 12 | Note | The decisions log is out of date (pending items; tested mode; "kept unseen"). | **Accept.** Update the log. |
