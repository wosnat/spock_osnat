The question

Two parts, answered gene by gene for the high-light-inducible (hli) genes:

  1. Do they respond to light? (light vs dark, how much light, UV)
  2. Or is it a general stress response unrelated to light? (nitrogen, phosphorus, iron, CO2, salt, temperature, phage and other stresses)

The light experiments the KG holds compare light with darkness; only one pair (MED4 proteomics) compares high with low light. The MED4 light microarray results were seen before planning and count as exploratory. hli results in the non-light stresses stay unseen until the plan is set.

The analysis is in tested mode: the prediction, the control genes and the rule for reading each result are fixed before the hli genes are scored.
