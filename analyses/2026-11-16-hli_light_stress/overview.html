The question

Are the high-light-inducible (hli) genes of cyanobacteria part of the response to light stress (excess light, UV)? We answer gene by gene, across every strain in the knowledge graph that has suitable data.

The analysis is in tested mode: the prediction, the control genes and the rule for reading each result are fixed before the hli genes are scored.
