Does the ntcA transcript change on urea? Both critic passes done — awaiting your approval

Proposal overview · analysis 2026-11-03-ntca_urea_response · source: proposal.md

Is the ntcA transcript level different when Prochlorococcus is acclimated to urea as its sole nitrogen source instead of ammonium? Readout: the ntcA transcript only (not NtcA activity on its targets). Scope: steady-state, N-replete cultures; not a test of an N-stress response. Hypothesis: ntcA is up on urea. Null: no change.

Data — Tolonen et al. 2006, Mol Syst Biol (all microarrays, all detected genes)

Every experiment is compared with log-phase cultures on N-replete Pro99 (ammonium). The strains are read separately; fold-change sizes are never compared across strains. Within a strain, the values are shown side by side for context, with no test between them.

Detectability: ntcA moves under N deprivation

Pass: at ≥ 1 time point used, ntcA is significantly up, or has log2FC > 0 in the top 25 % of genes by size of change. The paper's significance call is a list and does not follow fold-change size, which is why the size option is there. A pass means the probe responds, not that the array can pick up a small urea-sized change.
