# ntcA transcript response to urea in Prochlorococcus

## Question

Does the transcript level of *ntcA*, the global nitrogen regulator of cyanobacteria, differ when *Prochlorococcus* is acclimated to urea as its sole nitrogen source compared with ammonium? We examine two strains, the high-light-adapted MED4 and the low-light-adapted MIT9313.

## Background

In cyanobacteria, NtcA activates genes for the uptake and assimilation of alternative nitrogen sources and is itself induced under nitrogen limitation. Tolonen et al. (2006) profiled the transcriptomes of MED4 and MIT9313 acclimated to urea or to ammonium-replete medium. Here we ask whether *ntcA* itself differs between these steady states, using the urease and urea-transporter genes as a positive control.
