About 35 seconds. The Spock knowledge graph is one connected database for our marine microbes, with Prochlorococcus, Synechococcus and Alteromonas at the core. It holds about 330 thousand genes from 123 organisms, curated from 60 papers. That is 340 experiments and close to 360 thousand individual expression results, plus more than 3,000 metabolites. You don’t query it by hand: you ask Claude in plain English, and it queries the graph for you.
