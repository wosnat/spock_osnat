About 35 seconds. The measured data comes in four kinds that answer different questions. Each card shows how many datasets there are, and how many data points they hold. Expression: 193 experiments with almost 360 thousand gene results, each with a fold change, a p-value and a timepoint. Gene metrics: 302 columns that papers report about genes, like whether a gene is rhythmic. Gene clusters: 26 published co-expression analyses. And metabolomics: 14 assays measured on compounds, not genes. You don’t need to know which layer to use. Ask in plain English; Claude picks the tools, and every claim traces back to a query.
