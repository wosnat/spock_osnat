Not in the KG

  • Protein–protein interactions
  • SNPs and genome variants (one reference genome per strain)
  • Non-coding RNAs: tRNA, rRNA, sRNA
  • Transcript isoforms
  • 3-D protein structure

If the KG cannot answer, Claude should say so and mark it as a gap.

Three habits for working with it

  1. Genes are locus tags, not names. The name glnA matches 119 genes across the KG's strains; PMM0920 is exactly one, glnA in MED4. Spock resolves names first and reports locus tags.

  2. Every claim traces to a query. Findings are tagged [KG], [interpretation] or [gap].

  3. Read the experiment design. Check what each treatment was compared against, and at which timepoint, before you compare fold changes across studies.
