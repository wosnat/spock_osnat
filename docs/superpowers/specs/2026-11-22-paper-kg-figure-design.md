## Purpose
Accompanies the paper's KG section. One message: **concepts the KG makes possible**.
"What's in the KG" (schema, counts, growth, quality gate) goes to the overview figure,
not here.

## Constraints (from the researcher)
- **Illustrative.** No real results claimed; each card shows the *shape* of an answer.
  We are not solving the question. Example values (callout) are made up and labelled so.
- **General audience.** Plain words; no species, strain or gene names.
- **Not the stress-signature topic** (that is the walkthrough chapter's example).
- Terms allowed: ortholog, log2FC, padj.

## Content
Story strip (top): phytoplankton ("makes food from light") → sugars, amino acids… →
partner bacterium ("lives on what it releases"); dashed return "…and something back?"

Five cards, each = title · question in quotes · a 2–3-link walk drawn as a tiny graph with
named links · answer shape · red "against:" line.

| # | Title | Walk | Answer shape | Against |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | One partner, or any? | genes → changed in → each coculture study | shared by all partners, or unique to one | one experiment read as the rule |
| 2 | In other strains too? | gene → ortholog in → other strains → changed in | same response across strains, or one strain's quirk | one strain standing for all |
| 3 | What was overlooked? | changed in, never "named in" (paper) | pale = genes no paper names: new candidates | the paper's narrative |
| 4 | From molecules to genes | phytoplankton release gene ▲ → moved by → molecule measured in the water (exometabolomics) → moved by → bacterium uptake gene ▲ | two organisms, genes and molecules, in one walk | claims resting on one kind of data |
| 5 | What do they do? | genes → has function → part of pathway | the response summed up by function, e.g. mostly uptake | a story built from hand-picked genes |

Callout strip (bottom): "Every link carries its numbers and its source" — gene —
*changed in: log2FC 2.6 · padj 0.001 · rank 12 · from Table S2 of the paper* — study.
Against: claims without numbers.
